# Moorosi Agricultural High School

> **Invest Through Land**

A modern, responsive school website for Moorosi Agricultural High School, located at Fiva Location, Ludidi A/A, Maluti in the Eastern Cape, South Africa.

## About

Moorosi Agricultural High School is a public agricultural secondary school established in **2017**, serving the Alfred Nzo West Education District. The school offers quality education from **Grades 8 to 12** with a focus on both academic excellence and practical agricultural training.

As a **No-Fee Public School**, we are committed to making quality education accessible to all learners in our community.

- Agricultural field trips and farm visits
